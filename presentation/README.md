# Pitch deck

`VitaNatura365-pitch.pptx`: 8 slides for the 6-minute pitch, 3 speakers with 2 slides each.
Speaker notes with timings and talking points are on every slide (View > Notes in PowerPoint).

1. Intro: slogan
2. Speaker 1: the need (full 3 months, empty 6)
3. Speaker 1: stretching feasts into weeks (St. Blaise Week, Ston oyster season)
4. Speaker 2: new activities and what we build on the ground
5. Speaker 2: local jobs and income (photo safari, harvests)
6. Speaker 3: audience (empty nesters first) and influencer marketing
7. Speaker 3: EU funds and feasibility
8. Closing: conclusions and the only QR code, left up during questions

Design follows the academic-pptx and presentation-master skill rules: every title is a full sentence,
about 40 words per slide, one main visual, no price list.

Photos come from `web/public/img` and `presentation/img` (deck-only photos), credited on the last slide.
Icons from Phosphor (same as the app).

To change the text or design, edit `build-deck.js` and rebuild:

```bash
cd presentation
npm install
node build-deck.js
```
